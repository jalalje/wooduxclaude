About Woodux

Woodux is a practical reference library for people who want to build useful things from wood, set up a workshop, or plan an outbuilding without guesswork.

What we publish

Our guides cover beginner projects, tools, lumber, cut lists, joinery, sheds, workshop setup and self-sufficiency. We focus on clear explanations, realistic trade-offs and the questions that matter before you spend money or make a cut.

How recommendations work

Woodux does not sell products directly. When a guide mentions an external retailer, publisher or course provider, readers should evaluate the current price, availability and terms for themselves. If a commercial relationship changes, we will disclose it on the relevant page.

Safety note

Woodworking involves sharp tools, electricity, dust and structural loads. Treat every guide as general information, follow manufacturer instructions and local building rules, and use appropriate protective equipment.
