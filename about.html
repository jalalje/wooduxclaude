Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Is Woodux free?

Yes. Woodux publishes free woodworking guides designed to help beginners and home builders make better choices before they spend money.

Do I need a big shop to start woodworking?

No. A small area, a few essential tools, and a good first project are enough to learn valuable skills without overspending.

What should I read first?

Start with the beginner guide, then move into the tool buying guides and workshop setup pages so you build the right foundation before investing.
