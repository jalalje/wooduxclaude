Frequently asked questions

Is Woodux free?

Yes. Woodux publishes free practical guides for home woodworkers, builders and DIYers.

Does Woodux sell plans or products?

No. Woodux is an independent guide site. Some pages link to external retailers, publishers or course providers, but we do not sell products directly.

Are the guides suitable for beginners?

Yes. Start with the beginner projects and essential tools guides, then use the topic pages when you are ready for a larger build.
