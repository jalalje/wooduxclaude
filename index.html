Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Is Woodux free?

Yes. Woodux publishes independent guides on woodworking, workshop setup, and building projects. All content is free to read and use. Some links throughout the site are recommendations to external retailers and tool suppliers—we may earn a small commission if you make a purchase through those links, but it doesn't affect the price you pay.

Do I need experience to start woodworking?

No. Woodux guides are specifically designed for complete beginners with no prior experience. Start with the "Woodworking for Beginners: Where to Start" guide to learn the fundamentals and basic skills you'll use for almost any project.

How often is Woodux updated?

Guides are reviewed quarterly and updated as needed to reflect new tools, techniques, and reader feedback. New guides are added based on the questions we receive and emerging topics in woodworking and workshop setup.

Are the tool recommendations unbiased?

Yes. Woodux recommends tools based on value, reliability, and suitability for beginners—not brand partnerships. Each guide includes multiple options at different price points so you can choose what works for your budget.
