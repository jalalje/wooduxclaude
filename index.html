Are the outbound links on this site affiliate links?▼

Not currently. We are not affiliates of the companies linked from this site yet. If that changes for a given company, the disclosure on that page will be updated to say so.

Are these guides suitable if I only have basic power tools?▼

Yes. Most of the projects and skills covered on this site are written for basic hand and portable power tools — a circular saw, drill, and pocket-hole jig — without requiring commercial machinery.

How do cut lists save money on lumber?▼

Standard lumber comes in fixed board lengths. A well-made cut list shows how to nest cuts on each board to minimize leftover scrap and keep your lumber bill as low as possible.
