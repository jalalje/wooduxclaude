What a 60-Day Money-Back Guarantee Actually Covers

Digital woodworking and homesteading guides are almost always sold through ClickBank, a payment processor that has handled online transactions since 1997. Its 60-day guarantee shows up on nearly every product page in this space, but few buyers actually understand how the refund process works before they need it.

Who issues the refund

ClickBank, not the individual creator, processes the payment and the refund. That matters because it means a refund request goes through a standardized system rather than depending on one small business responding to an email. Requests are typically submitted through the order confirmation email or ClickBank's own customer support portal.

What "60 days" actually means

The 60-day window starts from the purchase date, not from when you first open or use the product. That gives enough time to actually work through a plan or two before deciding, rather than a same-day impulse trial window like many shorter guarantees offer.

What to check before you buy

  • Confirm the product page states ClickBank as the retailer — this is usually in the footer or checkout page.
  • Keep your order confirmation email; it contains the order number a refund request needs.
  • Note the purchase date somewhere separate from your inbox, in case the confirmation email is filtered to spam.

Why this matters more for digital products

With a physical tool or lumber purchase, you can usually judge fit before you fully commit. A digital plan library or guide is different — you cannot fully evaluate it until you have opened it. A real, standardized 60-day guarantee is what makes that kind of purchase a reasonable one instead of a leap of faith.
